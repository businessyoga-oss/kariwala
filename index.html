Why Kariwala

Built on Strong Foundations.
Driven by Purpose.

Since 1989, Kariwala Industries has grown from a single facility in Kolkata to a global manufacturing partner trusted by brands across 40+ countries. Our foundation is stability — reliable processes, skilled teams, and systems that have been refined over three decades.

But what sets us apart isn't just what we make — it's how we make it. We believe a business thrives when it balances commercial ambition with genuine care for people and planet. This philosophy, inspired by our Chairman's work on conscious business practices, runs through every decision we take.

How We Work

Seven Principles That Guide Our Business

Every dimension of our business is kept in balance — from the shop floor to the boardroom.

Strong Foundations

35+ years of operational stability — reliable systems, tested processes, and a resilient supply chain that global brands trust.

Creative Excellence

In-house design and sampling teams who turn concepts into products — from initial sketch to finished prototype within days.

Decisive Leadership

A management team that leads from the factory floor, not just the boardroom. Quick decisions, transparent communication.

Deep Relationships

Long-standing partnerships with customers, suppliers, and communities. Many of our client relationships span over a decade.

Honest Practices

What we say, we do. Full compliance, certified processes, and transparent reporting. Proof over promises.

Forward Vision

Investing in technology, sustainable materials, and new capabilities — always looking ahead while staying grounded.

Purpose Beyond Profit

Community healthcare, women's empowerment, environmental stewardship — because a business should contribute more than it consumes.
