As an avid Yoga practitioner, I have long experienced the transformative power of its principles in my personal and professional life. Business Yoga is my attempt to share that journey, blending the ancient wisdom of Yoga with modern business practices.

It offers a holistic approach to leadership, team building, and organizational success. While many associate Yoga with physical postures, its philosophy runs much deeper — encompassing timeless principles of discipline, balance, clarity, and purpose.

Yama — The Principles

The ethical foundation of how we conduct business. Truthfulness, non-harm, non-stealing, moderation, and non-attachment form the moral compass that guides every decision at Kariwala.

Niyama — The Discipline

Internal practices of purity, contentment, austerity, self-study, and surrender. In business, this translates to maintaining clean processes, honest growth, and continuously learning.

Asana — The Posture

Stability and comfort in our physical workspace and operations. The right structure, environment, and systems that allow our people to do their best work.

Pranayama — Life Force

The conscious management of energy — resources, cash flow, time, and attention. Breathing life into the organization with intention rather than compulsion.

Pratyahara — Letting Go

Withdrawing from distractions. Focusing on what truly matters, saying no to what doesn't serve our purpose, and not chasing every trend.

Dharana — Concentration

Single-pointed focus on our core mission. When an organization can concentrate its energy, extraordinary things become possible.

Dhyana — Meditation

Deep, sustained reflection on our purpose and direction. Strategic thinking that goes beyond quarterly numbers to ask the deeper questions.

Samadhi — The Pinnacle

When purpose and action become one. A state where the business operates in flow — balanced, conscious, and fulfilling its highest potential.

The Outcome

Samadhi

When purpose and action become one.

Samadhi is not a destination. It is a state of being where the business operates in harmony — balanced between growth and responsibility, between ambition and purpose, between profit and people.
