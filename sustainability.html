Empowering Our People

The Strength Behind Our Products

Environmental Responsibility

Sustainable by Choice,
Not by Chance

Our primary materials — jute, cotton, canvas — are natural and biodegradable. We actively reduce our environmental footprint through responsible sourcing, energy efficiency, water conservation, and waste management.

  • Priority use of natural, biodegradable, and recycled materials
  • Energy-efficient production processes
  • Water conservation and responsible discharge
  • Waste segregation, recycling, and minimisation
  • Compliance with REACH, SVHC, and international environmental standards

Our Philosophy

Business Yoga — A Conscious Approach

Our approach to sustainability isn't drawn from a textbook — it comes from a philosophy our Chairman, Anil Kariwala, has developed over decades of practice and reflection. In his book Business Yoga, he describes how seven dimensions of a business — its foundation, creativity, leadership, relationships, transparency, vision, and purpose — must be kept in balance, much like the seven energy centres described in yogic tradition.

He also outlines eight disciplines — ethical conduct, internal discipline, physical stability, energy management, focused attention, withdrawal from distractions, deep reflection, and harmony — that together create a framework for running a business consciously.

This isn't something we put on a website and forget. It genuinely shapes how we hire, how we treat our teams, how we choose our materials, and how we think about our role in the world.

"A business should contribute more than it consumes."

— Anil Kariwala, Founder & Chairman
