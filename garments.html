Our Capabilities

End-to-End Garment Manufacturing

From pattern development to finished carton — we handle the complete production cycle in our own facilities, giving you full visibility and control.

  • CAD-based pattern making, grading, and marker planning
  • Automated spreading and cutting with manual precision finishing
  • Multiple sewing lines — both speciality and bulk capacity
  • Embroidery, screen printing, heat transfer, and special finishes
  • In-house lab for fabric testing, colour fastness, and shrinkage
  • Pressing, packing, labelling, and barcoding per buyer specifications

Quality Assurance

What We Promise, We Deliver

Quality isn't a department — it's built into every stage of production. From incoming fabric inspection to final audit, every garment passes through multiple checkpoints.

Inline Inspection

Quality checks during production — not just at the end. Issues caught early mean fewer rejects and faster delivery.

Pre-Shipment Audit

Final inspection per AQL standards before any order leaves our facility. Buyer-nominated third-party audits welcomed.
